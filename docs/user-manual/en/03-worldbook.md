# Story bible and characters

A story bible keeps characters, places and rules linked to a manuscript. You can write without one and add it later.

Open **Characters** from the writing workspace. Choose **New character**, edit the name and the Background, Personality, Appearance and Other fields. Edits are saved through the existing story bible. **Mentioned in chapters** helps you return to relevant text.

Link a story bible through **Link story bible** in the chapter sidebar. Replacing or removing a link may require reviewing existing scene references; read the confirmation before proceeding. A missing story bible is not proof that the manuscript itself has been lost.

From **My books → Story Bible**, choose a section: World, Story, Characters or Writing rules. Edit a field directly; its saved indicator confirms the change. **Draft section** prepares AI suggestions for review, not an automatic replacement of your notes.

Use **Sources** in the same header to add reference files, read archived text or remove a reference from this book. Adding sources to a book without a story bible creates and links one when you confirm. **Entries** opens the detailed editor, including groups and advanced reference rules. Click **Save entry** after editing there.

Changing the interface language never translates character names, group names or author-written fields. Existing Chinese entry names remain as written. The common sources, settings and entry controls support English; map tools and some service errors may still show Chinese. Keep a [workspace backup](./07-settings.md) before reorganizing substantial material.
